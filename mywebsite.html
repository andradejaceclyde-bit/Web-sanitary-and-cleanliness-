About Our Platform

Learn more about our sanitation monitoring project.

Introduction

The Sanitation Monitoring Platform is an online system designed to address unmonitored cleanliness and sanitation issues in comfort rooms and classrooms at Minuyan National High School.

Purpose

This platform provides students, teachers, and staff with an accessible way to report sanitation concerns and monitor whether these issues have been resolved or remain unaddressed.

Objectives

• Provide an accessible reporting platform.
• Identify the locations of sanitation problems.
• Monitor the status of submitted reports.
• Encourage cleanliness and responsibility.

Our Mission

To promote a cleaner, safer, and healthier learning environment through accessible technology and community participation.
